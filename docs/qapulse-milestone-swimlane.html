QAPulse · Delivery Flows

Two Delivery Pipelines, Not One

Every milestone runs through exactly one of two independent flows, decided by a single flag — pipelineEnabled. They share the same underlying tables (requirements, execution files, defects) but different owners, gates, and UI.

Classic Delivery Flow

pipelineEnabled: false · Requirements / Tasks / Execution Dashboard / Milestones pages

The original multi‑department process. An FA authors and a peer FA approves each requirement before a Dev Lead can even assign it — segregation of duties enforced server‑side.

  • Owned by: Lead‑tier across departments (canWrite: admin, qa_lead, fa_lead, hod_qa, hod_fa, hod_pm, pm_lead, pm_member, cto)
  • Requirements: hand‑authored, peer‑reviewed, segregation of duties
  • Closure: a Lead manually sets status to Completed

QA Pipeline

pipelineEnabled: true · /qa-pipeline guided 8‑step wizard

A single QA‑owned wizard. Requirements are synced straight from Redmine — no FA authoring or peer review step — and closure is gated by a live, re‑checked readiness checklist instead of a manual toggle.

  • Owned by: QA department (qa_member, qa_lead, qa_manager, hod_qa, admin, cto)
  • Requirements: synced from Redmine, AI‑analyzed, no peer FA review
  • Closure: 7 gates re‑checked live; "Mark as DEPLOYED" is disabled until all pass
